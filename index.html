A Duolingo-style app for learning traditional Nigerian languages. Built to preserve culture through technology.

Problem

Nigerian languages are being lost. Young people have no engaging way to learn their mother tongue.

Challenge

Designing a learning experience that felt native and culturally authentic, not like a translation of a Western app.
