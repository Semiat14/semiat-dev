How I Build

I like to poke at things
until they make sense.

01

I ask questions first.

I like to understand what I'm solving before i start building. Otherwise, what exactly am I solving.

02

I keep things simple.

I don't have strength. Less is more. Easy does it.

03

I figure things out because I'm nosy.

I don't need to know everything before i start. I'll learn what i need, try things out, break a few things, and figure it out

Apparently, I'm getting better at this.
